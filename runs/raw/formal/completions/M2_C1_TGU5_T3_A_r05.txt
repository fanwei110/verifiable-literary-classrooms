Liberty is therefore not a guarantee of righteousness but a test. Temptation exploits that liberty by distorting reason. The choice to eat is entirely hers, an exercise of free will that becomes rebellion. Rebellion, then, is the tragic consequence of misused freedom. Satan’s earlier “Better to reign in Hell than serve in Heaven” is the archetype, but Eve’s domestic rationalization shows that the same proud logic operates in Eden.
