The hallucinated dagger, hovering and luring him toward the deed, embodies a conscience already stained by the thought of blood. Shakespeare demonstrates that prophecy acts as a catalyst, not fate. Once awakened, ambition poisons reason, and guilt materializes immediately—no ocean can wash it clean.
