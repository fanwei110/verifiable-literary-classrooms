Yet the plot slowly dismantles both the original financial logic of marriage and the faulty first impressions it spawns. Darcy learns humility; Elizabeth learns to see beyond wealth and pride. By the end, their union represents a marriage that corrects initial misjudgments, integrating true feeling with a realistic awareness of worldly station.
