Macbeth’s psyche unravels, haunted by visions and paranoia. Ambition promised power, but delivers torment. Prophecy acts not as a guarantee but a temptation, pushing Macbeth toward his downfall. This reveals his entrapment in guilt—there’s no redemption, only continuation. Lady Macbeth, once ambitious, cannot escape her conscience either, sleepwalking and scrubbing at invisible blood.
